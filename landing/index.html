THE THESIS

A vertically integrated industrial group, built for the UAE's construction decade.

The UAE's northern Emirates are entering their fastest construction cycle on record — driven by Ras Al Khaimah's tourism-led infrastructure program, Fujairah's port expansion, and more than forty thousand new residential units in Dubai in 2025 alone. Petravex is purpose-built to serve this demand. One holding, four operating companies, one site, one integrated margin.

I.

Vertical integration

One site, four revenue streams. The grinding station feeds the block plant, the trading arm captures the margin, the transport fleet closes the logistics loop. Competitors cannot replicate it.

II.

Institutional capital discipline

Twenty million US dollars deployed against a fixed scope. Two protected reserves covering licensing risk and collection lag. No hidden costs. Every line item accounted for.

III.

Self-funded expansion

The five-year roadmap — ready-mix, cement packaging, precast — is funded entirely from project cash flow. The initial capital is the only capital required.

THE FLAGSHIP ASSET

The 150 TPH Vertical Roller Mill clinker grinding station, RAKEZ.

The technical anchor of the group. A greenfield Vertical Roller Mill clinker grinding station, specified at 150 tons per hour, installed on a 30,000 square-metre plot in the Al Hulaila Industrial Zone of the Ras Al Khaimah Economic Zone. Designed to produce OPC 52.5 and CEM II cement at a Blaine fineness of 3,600 cm²/g, operating up to 3,800. Commissioning scheduled for the first quarter of 2027.

OPERATIONS

Four companies. One group. One integrated margin.

01 / 04

Petravex Clinker Grinding

150 TPH Vertical Roller Mill grinding station in RAKEZ.

  • OPC 52.5 and CEM II production
  • Clinker imported via RAK, Fujairah, Jebel Ali
  • Commissioning Q1 2027
02 / 04

Petravex Manufacturing

Fully automated Chinese block plant operating on the same site as the grinding station.

  • Hollow block, interlock, solid block, kerbstone
  • Up to 17,000 units per day
  • Captive aggregate supply from the group quarry feed
03 / 04

Petravex Contracting & Décor

Interior fit-out and contracting operations targeting commercial and residential developers.

  • Fit-out and finishing works
  • Preferential material pricing via the group trading arm
  • Serving UAE-wide projects
04 / 04

Petravex Transport

An independent logistics entity from day one, serving the group internally and external clients commercially.

  • 60-ton tippers, flatbeds, supervisory fleet
  • Inter-emirate delivery
  • Revenue-generating from the first operational day

A fifth entity — Petravex Trading — operates from day one as the commercial distribution arm, accelerating revenue before plant commissioning.
